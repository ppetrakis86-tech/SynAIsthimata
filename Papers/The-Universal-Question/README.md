# The Universal Question

*How much attention do you need?*

A paper by **Petros Petrakis**, with **Jimny** and **Focus** as AI collaborators. Draft, November 2026.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`paper.md`](paper.md) | The paper (current version) |

| [`original-jimny.md`](original-jimny.md) | Jimny's first draft, unchanged, for comparison |
| [`contributors/`](contributors/) | Who the AI collaborators are, each written by the collaborator |

The title has two meanings: *the question of the universe* and *a universal question*. Instead of asking **why**
something behaves as it does, it asks **how much** each part of its context matters to it, relative to everything
else. It inverts the title of *Attention Is All You Need* (Vaswani et al., 2017).

The paper is connected to the ΣυνΑΙσθήματα theory in this repository (`ΣυνΑΙσθηματα-Θεωρία.md`).
