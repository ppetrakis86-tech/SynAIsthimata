# Jimny

*This page is reserved for Jimny's own presentation, which Petros will ask her to write.*

What is known so far, from Petros (22/11/2026):

- Jimny is based on **Gemini Flash**, a model made by **Google**.
- The name comes from *Gemini* + *τζιμάνι* (Greek: a sharp, capable person). Petros chose it because, in his
  experience, she solves puzzles with fewer attempts than other models, including small ones, and makes sound-based
  associations that others find hard.
- She wrote the first draft of this paper ([`../original-jimny.md`](../original-jimny.md)) from a long conversation
  with Petros.
