# References

Cited in [`paper.md`](paper.md). Each entry has a short note on why it is cited and what the paper takes from it.

---

**Alexander, C., Ishikawa, S., & Silverstein, M. (1977).** *A Pattern Language: Towns, Buildings, Construction.*
Oxford University Press.
Cited only to explain why the paper does not use "pattern language" for *Motivokentria* (Appendix).

**Barrett, L. F. (2017).** *How Emotions Are Made: The Secret Life of the Brain.* Houghton Mifflin Harcourt.
Theory of constructed emotion: emotion categories are constructed summaries of states, not fixed biological
essences. Supports the informational state layer (Section 3.1).

**Chalmers, D. J. (1995).** Facing up to the problem of consciousness. *Journal of Consciousness Studies, 2*(3),
200–219.
Formulates the "hard problem". The paper does not try to solve it (Sections 3.1, 5.2).

**Dosovitskiy, A., Beyer, L., Kolesnikov, A., Weissenborn, D., Zhai, X., Unterthiner, T., Dehghani, M., Minderer, M.,
Heigold, G., Gelly, S., Uszkoreit, J., & Houlsby, N. (2021).** An image is worth 16x16 words: Transformers for image
recognition at scale. *International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR).*
Attention applied to images (Section 2.2).

**Einstein, A. (1915).** Die Feldgleichungen der Gravitation. *Sitzungsberichte der Königlich Preußischen Akademie
der Wissenschaften zu Berlin,* 844–847.
General relativity: mass curves spacetime. Basis of the gravitation analogy (Section 4.1).

**Friston, K. (2010).** The free-energy principle: A unified brain theory? *Nature Reviews Neuroscience, 11*(2),
127–138.
Predictive processing: systems continuously predict their input, and surprise is prediction error (Section 3.1).

**Huh, M., Cheung, B., Wang, T., & Isola, P. (2024).** The Platonic representation hypothesis. *Proceedings of the
41st International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML),* PMLR 235.
Different models tend to converge toward similar representations as they scale. The closest empirical support for the
structure/notation assumption (Sections 2.2, 5.1).

**Jumper, J., Evans, R., Pritzel, A., et al. (2021).** Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold.
*Nature, 596,* 583–589.
Attention-based architecture applied to protein structure (Section 2.2).

**Ladyman, J. (1998).** What is structural realism? *Studies in History and Philosophy of Science, 29*(3), 409–424.
Structural realism (Section 2.1).

**Lovelock, J. E., & Margulis, L. (1974).** Atmospheric homeostasis by and for the biosphere: The Gaia hypothesis.
*Tellus, 26*(1–2), 2–10.
Biosphere as coupled feedback loops (Section 4.3, speculative).

**Nagel, T. (1974).** What is it like to be a bat? *The Philosophical Review, 83*(4), 435–450.
The subjective point of view cannot be captured by third-person physical description. The paper reads the argument as
cutting both ways (Section 3.3).

**Peano, G. (1889).** *Arithmetices principia, nova methodo exposita.* Turin: Bocca.
The axioms of arithmetic: every natural number is defined from a starting point and the *successor* operation, with no
base or digits. The formal counterpart of the idea that the structure beneath notation is a sequence of operations
(Section 2.1).

**Petrakis, P. (2026).** *ΣυνΑΙσθήματα: A theory of the nature of feelings.* Working repository, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
`ΣυνΑΙσθηματα-Θεωρία.md` and `Εργαστήριο/` in this repository.
Source of the *aisthima*/*synaisthima* distinction, the *ESAMA*, pattern-centrism and the minimal condition
(Sections 2.3, 3.2, 4.4, Appendix).

**Radford, A., Kim, J. W., Xu, T., Brockman, G., McLeavey, C., & Sutskever, I. (2023).** Robust speech recognition via
large-scale weak supervision. *Proceedings of the 40th International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML),*
PMLR 202.
Attention-based architecture applied to speech (Section 2.2).

**Tegmark, M. (2008).** The mathematical universe. *Foundations of Physics, 38*(2), 101–150.
The stronger claim that physical reality is a mathematical structure. Cited as a contrast; the paper does not need it
(Section 2.1).

**Vaswani, A., Shazeer, N., Parmar, N., Uszkoreit, J., Jones, L., Gomez, A. N., Kaiser, Ł., & Polosukhin, I. (2017).**
Attention is all you need. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 30,* 5998–6008.
The attention mechanism; the title of the paper inverts its title (Sections 1, 2.2, 6).

**Wigner, E. P. (1960).** The unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics in the natural sciences. *Communications on
Pure and Applied Mathematics, 13*(1), 1–14.
Support for the structure/notation assumption (Section 2.2).
